About Wonderland

Built for first-timers

Wonderland gathers the fifty-odd surf breaks within driving distance of Agadir and filters them down to the ones that actually make sense for someone standing on a board for the first time. Every spot is rated for beginner suitability, with notes on bottom type, crowd levels, best season, and where to find a surf school. Research drawn from surf guides, school listings, and local knowledge — updated as conditions and access change.
