## 1. KITESURFING / WINGFOIL

**Agadir Bay** — one of Morocco's wind-sports destinations.
- Water: chop, small waves — suitable for beginners and intermediates.
- Wind: NW thermal wind, picking up in afternoons, ~3-5 Bft (12-20 knots).
- Best months: year-round, though less reliable than Essaouira or Dakhla.
- Note: not recommended July-August (beaches crowded, no designated kite area).
- Security boats in the water. Parking, toilets, beach bar, showers available at marina.
- Schools: Surf & Chill Agadir offers kitesurf lessons (beginners to advanced).
  - Prices: 1×2h €60, 3×2h €160, 6×2h €300 (equipment + wetsuit included).
  - Beginners recommended 3-5 sessions to learn basics.
- Also: wingfoil and paddleboard available at same schools.
- Nearby kite spots outside Agadir: Essaouira (best overall, summer season), Dakhla (year-round, protected lagoon), Oualidia Lagoon (flat water, ~180km north).

## 2. QUAD BIKING / ATV TOURS

Very popular in Agadir hinterland — off-road touring rather than technical enduro.
- Terrain: argan groves, eucalyptus forests, dry oued beds, small dunes, Berber villages.
- Typical format: 2-3 hour half-day tours; sunset rides are the favourite.
- No driving licence needed for guided tours; helmet and briefing provided.
- Prices (approximate):
  - Quad (solo): 300-600 MAD for 2-hour guided tour (~€25-50).
  - Buggy (2 seats): 600-1,200 MAD for half-day (~€50-100).
  - Budget options: from €15-20 for 1-2 hour tours (GetYourGuide, local operators).
- Common tour includes: hotel pickup, safety briefing, 1-2 hours riding, Berber village stop with mint tea, return.
- Operators: many local companies (Terre Mer Adventure, Ecolodge Adventures, Quad Timlaline, etc.).
- Best time: year-round; spring/autumn ideal; morning (cooler) or sunset (scenic).
- Combos: quad + sandboarding, quad + horse riding, quad + Crocodile Park, quad + Berber lunch.

## 3. CAMEL TREKKING

- Available as shore excursions and desert trips from Agadir.
- Common format: 1-2 hour beach camel rides (sunset or morning options).
- Often combined with quad biking or horseback riding tours.
- Examples: "Camel Ride Shore Excursion with Local Guide" — hotel pickup, beach walk, ~US$20-30 per person.
- More immersive: multi-hour desert camel treks into the Souss countryside.
- Budget: ~€15-30 for short beach rides; higher for longer desert treks.

## 4. PARAGLIDING / TANDEM FLIGHTS

**Agadir Parapente** — certified tandem paragliding, Douira (Dweira) Beach, south of Agadir.
- 10-15 minute tandem flights over the Atlantic Ocean and coastline.
- Pilots: 15+ years experience, certified, daily weather checks.
- Price: from €70 per flight (Discovery Pack ~900 MAD); longer flights up to €120+.
- Includes: hotel pickup/drop-off, safety equipment, GoPro video option.
- Age range: 4 to 94 years.
- Takeoff: Dweira Beach — sweeping coastline, ocean views.
- Other sites around Agadir region: Taghazout (Terre D'Ocean camping, 25km north), Agadir Kasbah (temporarily inaccessible — construction), Tafraoute (Anti-Atlas, grand scenery), Taroudant (Col du Tizi'n'Test).
- Paramotoring also available — similar tandem experience with motorized paraglider.

## 5. HIKING / NATURE WALKS

- **Paradise Valley (Taghazout area)** — popular day trip from Agadir: natural pools, waterfall, hiking to swimming spots. ~6-8 hours with guide. ~US$24 per person.
- **Atlas Mountains foothills** — quad biking tours often pass through hiking-accessible terrain; the Anti-Atlas and Lesser Atlas are reachable for day hikes.
- **Souss Massa National Park** — nature reserve south of Agadir, wildlife viewing (Dorcas gazelle, amphibians), coastal and dune habitats.
- Coastal walks: stretches of undeveloped coastline between Taghazout and Aglou (south).
- Towns for hiking base: Tafraoute (Anti-Atlas, rock formations, berber villages), Tiznit (south, gateway to coastal hikes).

## 6. BOAT TRIPS / FISHING CHARTERS

- **Half-day boat trips from Agadir Marina** (5 hours, ~9:30 AM start):
  - Fishing (equipment provided, crew assists beginners), swimming stop, grilled fish BBQ lunch on board.
  - ~€150 per person (fishing charter); budget boat trips ~€45-60 per person.
  - Operators: Agyadino Tours, Immersi Travel, GetAgadir, musement.
  - Includes: hotel pickup, air-conditioned transport to marina, lunch, drinks, fishing gear.
- **Deep sea fishing** — dedicated charters for experienced anglers, ~€45-150 per person.
- **Taghazout boat trips** — 4-hour fishing + BBQ lunch, departing from north of Agadir.
- Scenic coastal cruises also available (no fishing).

## 7. OTHER WATERSPORTS

- **Paddleboarding (SUP)** — calm flat water, available through surf schools (Surf & Chill Agadir offers it).
- **Kayaking** — also offered by surf schools as an add-on activity.
- **Jetski** — available as beach activity from Agadir (operators listed on excursion platforms).
- **Sandboarding** — on the dunes south of Agadir; often included in quad bike day packages.
- **Wakeboarding / wakesurf** — limited local info found; may be available through luxury hotel resorts.

## 8. GOLF
